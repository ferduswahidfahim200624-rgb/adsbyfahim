My 4-Pillar Contractor Google Ads System

1. High-Intent Emergency & Replacement Segmentation

An AC repair call and a full HVAC system replacement have completely different margins and CPAs. I separate campaigns and ad groups by service line (e.g., Emergency Roof Leak Repair vs. Full Roof Replacement, AC Repair Near Me vs. New Furnace Installation) and zip-code radius so you control budget allocation by profit margin.

2. The 400+ Contractor Negative Keyword Shield

Before spending dollar one, I upload my battle-tested contractor negative keyword lists blocking job hunters (hvac jobs, roofer salary, apprentice), DIYers (how to patch, home depot parts, manual, reddit), freebie seekers (government roof grant, free ac repair), and out-of-state searchers.

3. Closed-Loop CallRail, GTM & GoHighLevel Tracking

Over 70% of home service leads come via phone call. I configure CallRail Dynamic Number Insertion (DNI), Google Ads call extensions, and GTM Enhanced Conversions for Leads (including Stape.io server-side tracking) with minimum call-duration filters and GoHighLevel/CRM offline GCLID imports.

4. Location 'Presence Only' Lock & Ad Schedule Bidding

We lock location targeting strictly to Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations and align bid modifiers with the hours your dispatchers actually answer the phone.
