You must isolate high-value contingency case types (commercial truck crashes, motorcycle wrecks, wrongful death, and medical malpractice) into Exact and Phrase match ad groups, lock location targeting to Presence Only around major highway corridors and trauma centers, and feed signed contingency retainer data from Filevine, LeadDocket, or Clio back into Google Ads via offline GCLID imports.

The 4 Biggest Budget Leaks Inside Personal Injury Google Ads Accounts

  • Leak #1 — Insurance & Police Report Searches: Broad match triggers $120+ ads for "state farm accident claim phone number", "dmv crash report", and "who is at fault in rear end".
  • Leak #2 — Blended Case Types: Commercial 18-wheeler collisions (six- to seven-figure case value) get starved of budget because generic "car accident lawyer" queries consume the daily cap by 11:00 AM.
  • Leak #3 — Tracking Unqualified 15-Second Calls: When Google Ads counts every wrong number, existing client status check, or property-damage-only caller as a conversion, Smart Bidding learns to buy more unqualified calls.
  • Leak #4 — Lost Mobile Attribution on Safari/iOS: Over 80% of injured claimants search on iPhones; without first-party Stape.io server-side tracking and SHA-256 Enhanced Conversions, 25–35% of signed cases go unattributed.

Frequently Asked Questions for Personal Injury Attorneys

How do you prevent $150+ personal injury clicks from going to minor property-damage or non-injury searches?

I separate high-value case types (18-wheeler truck crashes, catastrophic injury, wrongful death, medical malpractice) into Exact and Phrase match ad groups and deploy a 500+ personal injury negative keyword shield blocking insurance claim phone numbers, police report downloads, minor scratch/dent repair, pro bono, and rival firm brand names.

Can you connect Google Ads to Filevine, LeadDocket, Clio Grow, or Lawmatics for signed-case tracking?

Yes. I capture hidden GCLID and SHA-256 Enhanced Conversions parameters on every mobile call and case-evaluation form via CallRail DNI and Stape server-side GTM, then sync your intake CRM so Google Smart Bidding receives offline conversions when a plaintiff signs a contingency fee agreement.

Why should a personal injury firm run Search + CallRail alongside Local Services Ads (LSA)?

Local Services Ads capture top-of-page phone calls, while intent-segmented Google Search ads let you control exact case types (such as commercial truck accidents or motorcycle crashes), custom landing page pre-qualification, and zip-code bid adjustments around major highways and trauma hospitals.
