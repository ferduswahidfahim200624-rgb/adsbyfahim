Why I Combine Data Analysis With Performance Ads

Hi, I'm Ferdous Wahid Fahim — working at the intersection of Data Analyst, Digital Marketer, Growth Marketer, and Performance Marketer. Over 4+ years and 100+ local and international clients, I saw the same root cause behind almost every underperforming ad account:

Business owners were blaming keywords, ad copy, or "rising CPCs," when the real problem was underneath the hood: broken data and conversion tracking. Double-counted thank-you pages, untracked phone calls, missing purchase values, or lost browser signals were training Google's Smart Bidding algorithm to optimize for the wrong people.
