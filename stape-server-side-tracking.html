Server-Side Tracking FAQ

Common Questions About Stape & GTM

What is included in your Stape.io server-side tracking setup?+

Every build includes provisioning your GTM Web and Server containers, configuring a first-party custom subdomain with Stape Custom Loader and Cookie Keeper, setting up GA4 server routing, Google Ads Conversion Tracking with SHA-256 Enhanced Conversions, Meta Pixel + Server CAPI with event_id deduplication, and full QA in Tag Assistant and Meta Events Manager.

Can you fix Shopify or WooCommerce Google Ads conversion tracking not working?+

Yes. I audit your Shopify Customer Events pixel / extensible checkout or WooCommerce dataLayer, remove duplicate GA4 and native tag conflicts, pass dynamic transaction_id, value, currency, and hashed customer data, and verify every checkout event in GTM Preview and GA4 DebugView.

How fast can you audit and rebuild a broken GTM and GA4 container?+

Most standalone GA4, GTM, Enhanced Conversions, and Stape.io server-side container rebuilds are completed, tested, and verified within 3 to 5 business days, starting with a free 30-minute diagnostic audit.

Will server-side tracking cause double-counting in Google Ads or Meta Ads?+

No. I enforce strict 1:1 event_id and transaction_id deduplication between Web and Server containers, pause redundant hardcoded pixels, and configure only one Primary conversion source per action in Google Ads.
