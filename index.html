FAQ

Common Questions

My account is already running ads. Why start with conversion tracking?+

Because Google's Smart Bidding (Target CPA / Maximize Conversions / PMax) optimizes toward whatever you tell it is a conversion. If page views, spam calls, or duplicate events are firing as conversions, you are paying Google to find more low-quality traffic. Fixing the signal first immediately improves lead quality and lowers CPA.

Can I hire you just for GA4, GTM, or Server-Side Tracking?+

Yes! I regularly take on standalone tracking & analytics projects — including GA4 Enhanced E-commerce, Google Ads Enhanced Conversions, Stape.io server-side containers, Meta CAPI, CallRail, GoHighLevel/CRM attribution, and Looker Studio dashboards.

How do you structure keywords and negative keywords?+

I organize campaigns around your specific services, profit margins, and target locations, grouping closely related keywords by search intent so the ad copy and landing page match what the buyer typed. I combine shared negative lists with weekly search-term pruning so you never pay for irrelevant DIY, job, or informational searches.

What monthly ad budget do you recommend?+

I've managed accounts spending from $1.5K–$3.5K/month up to $15K–$67K+. During our free audit, I'll look at your niche's CPCs and tell you honestly what budget is realistic to hit your lead or revenue target.
