Why Your GA4 & Google Ads Numbers Never Match (And How to Fix Double-Counting)

In 7 out of 10 audits I run, the ad account is either counting every page refresh of /thank-you as a new lead, or missing 40% of phone calls. Both destroy Smart Bidding efficiency.

Key Takeaways

  • Primary vs. Secondary Actions: Never set both a native Google Ads tag and an imported GA4 key event as Primary.
  • Transaction / Event ID Deduplication: Pass a unique event_id in the dataLayer so refreshes never double-count.
  • Enhanced Conversions: Enable SHA-256 hashed email & phone matching in GTM to recover cross-device conversions.

Direct Answer: Why GA4 & Google Ads Conversion Numbers Disagree

When Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Ads conversion numbers are drastically out of sync, the root cause is almost always having both a native Google Ads tag and an imported GA4 Key Event set to Primary, or firing a Google Tag Manager (GTM) trigger multiple times on page reloads. Even after fixing tracking bugs, a 5–15% difference is normal because Google Ads attributes conversions to the ad click date, while GA4 attributes them to the conversion event date across all channels.

Cause #1: Native Google Ads Tag + Imported GA4 Key Event Both Set to Primary

Google's Target CPA and Target ROAS algorithms optimize toward whatever you mark as a Primary Conversion Action. The #1 mistake I find during tracking audits is an account where a prospect submits one form, and Google Ads records 2 conversions:

  • Conversion 1 fires from the native Google Ads Conversion Tracking Tag in GTM.
  • Conversion 2 is imported automatically from a linked GA4 Key Event (such as generate_lead or purchase).

The Fix: Open Google Ads > Goals > Conversions > Summary. Keep the native Google Ads tag (paired with Enhanced Conversions) as Primary for bidding, and switch the imported GA4 key event to Secondary (Observe Only). That way you can still compare both columns in custom reports without inflating your conversion count.

Cause #2: Client-Side Duplicate Firing in GTM (Thank-You Reloads & Counting 'Every')

If a lead bookmarks or refreshes your /thank-you page on mobile, a basic page-view trigger will fire the conversion tag again. Worse, if the Google Ads conversion action is set to count Every instead of One, every reload inflates your reported leads.

  • For Lead Generation (Forms, Bookings, Calls): Edit the conversion action settings in Google Ads and set Count = One per ad click. In GTM, fire the tag on a verified form-submission dataLayer event (e.g., generate_lead) rather than a naked URL match.
  • For Shopify & WooCommerce eCommerce: Keep Count = Every (since a customer can place two real orders), but always pass a dynamic transaction_id (Order ID) in the dataLayer so Google Ads automatically deduplicates order-confirmation page refreshes.

Cause #3: Configure Google Tag Manager for Enhanced Conversions

Safari ITP, mobile browser switches, and ad-blockers strip URL click identifiers (gclid). When you configure Enhanced Conversions in Google Tag Manager, GTM captures first-party customer data (email address, phone number, first/last name) at the moment of form submit or checkout, hashes it with SHA-256, and matches it back to the signed-in Google account that clicked your ad.

Cause #4: Structural Differences You Should Not 'Fix'

Once double-counting is eliminated, expect three healthy differences between platforms:

  1. Click Date vs. Conversion Date: If a buyer clicks your ad on November 20 and submits a lead form on November 24, Google Ads credits November 20 (by default), while GA4 credits November 24. Use the Conversions (by conv. time) column in Google Ads to compare apples to apples.
  2. Attribution Scope: Google Ads only evaluates Google Ads touchpoints; GA4 shares credit across Organic Search, Direct, Email, and Paid Social.
  3. Conversion Lookback Windows: A 90-day click lookback window in Google Ads will capture long-cycle B2B or legal leads that a 30-day window in GA4 drops.

5-Minute GTM & Tag Assistant QA Checklist

Before spending another dollar on Smart Bidding, run this diagnostic check: open tagassistant.google.com, submit a live test lead or test order, confirm the Conversion Linker fires on all pages, verify your Primary conversion tag fires once, and confirm the Enhanced Conversions parameter object passes a valid hashed email or phone value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do Google Ads and GA4 show different conversion numbers?

Google Ads and GA4 differ for two reasons: accidental double-counting (having both a native Google Ads conversion tag and an imported GA4 key event set to Primary for the same action) and normal structural differences (Google Ads reports by click date and last-ad-click, whereas GA4 reports by conversion event date across all channels).

How do I fix Google Ads double counting conversions?

Go to Google Ads > Goals > Conversions > Summary. Make sure only ONE conversion source per action is marked Primary (recommended: keep the native Google Ads tag with Enhanced Conversions as Primary and set the imported GA4 event to Secondary). Set lead actions to count 'One' instead of 'Every', and pass a unique transaction_id for eCommerce purchases.

What does 'Google Ads conversion tracking setup is incomplete' mean in GTM?

This diagnostic warning usually means your Google Ads Conversion Linker tag is missing in Google Tag Manager, Enhanced Conversions user-provided data (email/phone) is not mapped correctly from the dataLayer, or Consent Mode v2 signals are not firing before the conversion tag.
